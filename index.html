AIRe Lab @ CMU

CMU Artificial Intelligence and Reinforcement Learning Lab

We develop machines that act intelligently and autonomously in open-world scenarios, building reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making algorithms for AI systems in the intersection of language, robotics, software agents, science and engineering, and more.
